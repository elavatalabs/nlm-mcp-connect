What gets shared

NotebookLM has no official API, so Google offers no permission for it. The connector opens a fresh browser profile used only for this, sends that profile's Google sign-in to the MCP server over HTTPS, and deletes the profile. The server keeps it encrypted in Supabase Vault and refreshes it every 10 minutes. It never sees your password. To revoke it, sign that session out under Google Account > Security > Your devices, or remove the connector from your app.

What gets shared, and why there's no "Sign in with Google" button

NotebookLM has no official API, so Google offers no OAuth permission for it. The command opens a fresh Chrome profile used only for this, reads that profile's Google sign-in cookies after you sign in, sends them to the server over HTTPS, then deletes the profile. The server keeps the session encrypted in Supabase Vault and refreshes it every 10 minutes, so it doesn't expire. It never sees your password. To revoke access, sign that session out under Google Account > Security > Your devices.
